Project Overview

LibraryPro

Smart AI Powered Library Management System

Our Mission

LibraryPro is designed to revolutionize how educational institutions manage their book inventory, student borrowing records, and digital catalogs. By integrating modern web technologies and advanced Artificial Intelligence, we aim to eliminate manual overhead, provide actionable analytics, and offer students an unparalleled learning experience.

Key Objectives
  • Automation: Streamline book issuing, returns, and waitlist queues.
  • Intelligence: Leverage Gemini AI to provide instant book summaries and semantic search.
  • User Experience: Deliver a premium, commercial-grade interface that works seamlessly on all devices.
  • Reliability: Maintain a secure, optimized, and robust backend architecture.
